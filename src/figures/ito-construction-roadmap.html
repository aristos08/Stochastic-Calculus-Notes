Elementary integrands
\(\displaystyle H=\sum_{i=1}^n Z_{i-1}\mathbf{1}_{(T_{i-1},T_i]}\)
Resulting stochastic processes
\(\displaystyle H\cdot M=\left\{\int_0^t H_s\,dM_s\right\}_{t\ge 0}\)

1. Give the integrands a norm and a measurable structure.

2. Identify \(L^2\)-bounded martingales with their terminal values in \(L^2(\F_\infty)\).

3. Show that the elementary integrands are dense in the completed integrand space \(L^2(M)\).
